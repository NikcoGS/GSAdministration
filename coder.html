House rules — the judgement calls behind the coding
  1. Wallet top-ups are shipping cost. GoPay, OVO and ShopeePay top-ups fund GoSend and Gojek deliveries, so they go straight to 660222 Biaya Ekspedisi as an expense rather than being parked on the balance sheet. One thing to watch: if a store also submits the individual deliveries it paid for out of that wallet, the cost is in the books twice — a top-up and the spend it funds cannot both be claimed.
  2. A reimbursement with no description cannot be coded. Lines reading only “reimburse petty cash” land in 660306 Biaya Operasinal Lainnya and are flagged. Get the underlying receipts before posting, especially anything over the threshold — 660306 should stay small, not become the biggest account on the page.
  3. Equipment is tested against the capitalisation threshold. Cables, adaptors and small accessories are 660211 Sarana Kantor. A laptop, printer or monitor at or above the threshold belongs in 110705 Aset Tetap — Perlengkapan Kantor and gets depreciated.
  4. Goods are not opex. Tees, grips, shafts and anything that can be resold or moved between stores go to inventory (110200) or 550300 Pengiriman & Pengangkutan, not to supplies or expedition. Decide per line whether the item is consumed in-house or held for sale.
  5. A trip that carries goods is shipping, not transport. Jogja runs, Selatan to PIK, anything “to cust” — the journey exists to deliver an item, so it codes to 660222 Biaya Ekspedisi. 660103 Makanan & Transportasi is for a person getting to work or between sites with nothing to carry, like Aaron's daily fare.
  6. Vehicle: fuel vs maintenance vs equipment. Fuel, tolls, parking and EV charging go to 660202; car wash, service and parts to 660203; a physical charger or accessory bought for the car is equipment, so test it against rule 3.
  7. Every line still needs an entity. This COA serves CV GS, PT TKO, CV KBS and PT SGI. Premiere, Selatan, PIK and Batcave are detected and shown as a tag, but the journal entry needs the right company or analytic account or the cost lands in the wrong P&L.

Codes and names come from the Odoo export dated 15 October 2026. Re-export the chart of accounts if codes have changed since.
